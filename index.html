How to interpret this module

Not an alert system

Weather forecasts describe modeled atmospheric conditions, not guaranteed outcomes. Earthquake events are historical observations from the USGS feed; this platform does not predict earthquakes. For urgent local warnings, follow your official meteorological and disaster-management authorities.
